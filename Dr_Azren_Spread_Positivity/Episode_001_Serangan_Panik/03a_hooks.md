\* C has to carry the "international study, adults" qualifier inside the first 3 s, which slows the hook.

## Selected: B, with C's statistic as the second beat

- **Why B:** It shows the symptom in a situation almost every Malaysian adult knows (the commute home). It works **muted**, because the picture and the big words carry it. It makes no medical claim in the first 3 s, so nothing needs a caveat. And it sets up the episode's metaphor: car → **car alarm**.
- **C's statistic** follows at about 8 s as the "you're not alone" beat, where its footnote has room.
- **A** is kept as the TikTok on-screen title text option for an A/B test later.
