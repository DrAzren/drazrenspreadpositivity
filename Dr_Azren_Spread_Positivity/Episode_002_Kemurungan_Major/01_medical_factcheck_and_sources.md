## 3. Safety checklist

- [x] No diagnosis of the viewer ("mungkin", "hanya doktor boleh sahkan").
- [x] No promise of cure or of a timeline.
- [x] Self-help framed as a complement, explicitly "bukan pengganti rawatan".
- [x] No drug names, doses or advice to start or stop medication.
- [x] Red flag (self-harm thoughts) with **three** Malaysian routes, including 24-h Befrienders and 999 for immediate danger.
- [x] Non-stigmatising: "bukan malas, bukan lemah"; "masalah kesihatan sebenar". No "lemah iman" framing (it would invite religious debate; your call if you want a faith-sensitive line in future).
- [x] Grief vs depression, bipolar disorder, children/teens: **not covered** (too much for 85 s). The caption/description says the video is general education.

## 4. Safe messaging (suicide-adjacent content), per S7

- [x] No mention of methods, places or "how".
- [x] The words "bunuh diri" (suicide) are not spoken; "fikiran untuk mencederakan diri" (thoughts of harming yourself) is used. This is direct enough to be understood, not sensational.
- [x] Help-seeking is shown as normal and effective, with hope and recovery messages after the red flag.
- [x] Help lines are on screen long enough to read (≥ 4 s) and repeated in every platform caption.
- [x] Visual tone at the red flag: calm, teal (help) colour, not red alarm imagery; no distressing image of self-harm.
