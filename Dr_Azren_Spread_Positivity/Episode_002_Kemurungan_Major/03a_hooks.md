# Episode 002: three hooks (first 2–3 s)

The rule from the brief: no greeting, no self-introduction, problem on screen at frame 0, medically responsible, no invented statistics, no fear-bait.

| # | Type | Narration (BM) | On-screen headline | Frame-0 visual | Score /10 (stop power · accuracy · fit with the visual metaphor) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| A | Relatable symptom | "Dah tidur lapan jam… tapi bangun tetap rasa letih dan kosong?" | **TIDUR CUKUP, TETAP LETIH?** | Patient sitting up in bed, battery icon on 5 % | 7 · 8 · 6. Very relatable, but tiredness has many causes (anaemia, thyroid, sleep apnoea), so it risks implying depression is *the* reason |
| **B** ✅ | **Curiosity / lesser-known symptom** | **"Benda yang dulu buat anda gembira… sekarang rasa kosong?"** | **DULU SUKA, KINI KOSONG?** | Patient's favourite things (music, teh tarik) in colour on one side, **grey** on the other, grey rain cloud over her | **9 · 9 · 10**. Loss of interest is a *core* criterion many people don't recognise as depression. It sets up the colour-drain metaphor that carries the whole video |
| C | Fact / myth-buster | "Sedih biasa reda. Tapi kalau dah dua minggu tak hilang-hilang?" | **SEDIH ≠ KEMURUNGAN** | Calendar filling with grey days | 7 · 9 · 7. Accurate and useful, but it opens on a definition, which is weaker as a thumb-stopper |

## Choice: **B**

- **Retention:** the question invites self-reflection ("is that me?") within 2 s, and frame 0 already shows the problem visually (colour vs grey).
- **Medical responsibility:** anhedonia is a core DSM-5 / WHO feature (S1, S4), and the hook asks rather than diagnoses.
- **Visual payoff:** the colour-drain metaphor (DULU → KINI) pays off at the end when colour sweeps back in ("Warna dalam hidup anda boleh kembali"). That gives the video a beginning–end loop, which helps replays.
- **Sound:** a soft marker-scratch plus a low "deflate" tone as colour drains. Tasteful, not alarming.

Hook C's line "Sedih sekali-sekala itu normal" is kept inside the script (Scene 3).
